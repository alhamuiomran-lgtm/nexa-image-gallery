Support and privacy

Request help

Use the support channel supplied with your purchase or private RC delivery. Include theme/plugin versions, WordPress/PHP versions, the affected operation, reproducible steps and a redacted error message. Use staging access when troubleshooting; do not publish passwords, customer records or mail credentials.

Scope

Support scope, duration and update entitlement must be stated in the seller’s final listing and purchase terms. This candidate does not promise a response-time guarantee or marketplace support entitlement. For this private candidate, use the existing delivery conversation with CodeHavenHub; final public support details must be supplied before sale.

Requests and personal data

Limit Requests access to authorized administrators. Define a retention period appropriate to your business and applicable requirements; no automatic retention interval is promised here. Review WordPress privacy export/erasure tools and remove records through authorized administration workflows when appropriate.

Security reports

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through the supplied support channel. Provide a minimal staging reproduction without real customer information. Avoid posting active exploit details or credentials in a public support comment.

Consult the release test matrix and license manifest before using a release candidate commercially.
