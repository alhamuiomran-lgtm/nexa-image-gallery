Troubleshooting

The theme cannot be installed

Upload the inner installable theme ZIP. Check hosting upload limits and free disk space. Do not rename an outer commercial bundle and assume it is installable.

Studio is unavailable

Confirm Companion is installed and active, that your account has the required administration permissions, and that its version matches the theme download.

Import stops or a preview fails

Record the design, operation, time and exact message. Check storage, PHP limits and permissions. Retry on staging after resolving the cause. Do not reset the database or repeatedly import different designs to repair an unknown error.

An image is missing

Check the Media Library, the final asset manifest and the page’s selected attachment. Preview-only images may be excluded from the download. Replace missing images with eligible local media; do not paste private download tokens into public content.

A page reports an invalid block

Keep a revision/backup before recovery. Confirm that the matching Companion plugin is active, record the affected block and reopen a staging copy. Avoid converting entire pages to HTML just to hide the warning.

Mail is missing

Follow the SMTP guide and verify delivery in a controlled external inbox. A request listed in WordPress only proves that it was stored.

Diagnostic logs

Enable debugging on staging with error display off. Keep logs private and remove credentials and personal data before sharing an excerpt. Disable unnecessary debugging in production.

Consult the release test matrix and license manifest before using a release candidate commercially.
